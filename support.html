DON’T TOUCH Support

Support for the DON’T TOUCH phone-free challenge by Bizzyjo Projects.

How it works

The organizer creates a room in the iOS app and shares the invitation link. Participants join through their browser without installing the app. The organizer selects a mode and duration, then starts the challenge.

Common questions

  • A participant cannot join: open the newest invitation link in Safari or Chrome and check the internet connection.
  • Motion detection does not start: allow motion and orientation access when the device asks for permission.
  • The screen turns off: keep the browser open and temporarily increase the device’s automatic screen-lock time if needed.
  • A room no longer works: ask the organizer to create a new room and share its new invitation link.

Contact support

For help, bug reports, or questions, contact Bizzyjo Projects through the developer contact information on the DON’T TOUCH App Store page.
